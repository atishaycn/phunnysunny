About PhunnySunny

Founded 2026 in San Francisco

What we build

PhunnySunny builds AI creator tools. Our pipeline plans, scripts, renders, and publishes AI-made short-form video channels. Around it we ship tools for creators and developers: a skills marketplace at skills.phunnysunny.com, publishing tools for TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram, and DP Code, a web interface for coding agents.

Shipped

Real Ones is our free iPhone app on the App Store. Dark Mode Everywhere is an open-source browser extension. We build in public on X and GitHub.
