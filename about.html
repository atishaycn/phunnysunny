About PhunnySunny

Founded 2026 in San Francisco

What we build

PhunnySunny builds AI agents for short-form video. Studio plans, schedules, and publishes posts to YouTube, Instagram, and X. The Script Engine turns notes into scripts and threads, and the Video Engine renders scenes to video. You approve every post before it goes out. Studio and the engines are in early access; request access.

Why us

We run our own AI-made channels on these tools every day, so every feature starts as real work. We also ship Skills, a free catalog of agent skills, and Real Ones, our free iPhone app on the App Store.
