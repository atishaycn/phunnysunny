Data Deletion

Effective date: June 17, 2026

How To Request Deletion

To request deletion of information connected to a PhunnySunny project, email connect@phunnysunny.com or contact us through X or GitHub with the project name and the account or identifier you want removed.

Connected Platform Data

You can also revoke PhunnySunny access directly from the connected platform, such as Meta, Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, Google, or Apple. Revoking access prevents future use of that connection.

What We Delete

When a valid request is received, we delete stored access tokens, account identifiers, uploaded media, captions, logs, or other project data associated with the request, unless retention is required for security, legal, or platform compliance reasons.
